Biography & experience

An engineering career,
grounded in measurement.

Associate Professor in the Department of Measurements, Electrical Apparatus and Static Converters at POLITEHNICA Bucharest.

View academic CV

Research, teaching
and applied engineering.

My work brings together electrical engineering, experimental measurement and signal processing with advanced mathematics, statistics and artificial intelligence. My research interests include battery modelling, energy-storage diagnostics, power conversion and intelligent monitoring, using mathematical models and data analysis to support data-driven decision-making.

Alongside university research and teaching, my professional experience spans utility-network design, engineering-team leadership and industrial research and development. I apply advanced statistical methods and artificial intelligence to interpret complex data, evaluate uncertainty and inform decisions across engineering and wider societal applications.
