FARCROSS

FAcilitating Regional CROSS-border Electricity Transmission through Innovation

Project overview

FARCROSS brought together energy-system stakeholders to demonstrate hardware and software that increase the use of transmission assets and support regional cooperation. Its scope includes dynamic line rating, wide-area protection, renewable-generation forecasting, reserve optimisation and regulatory barriers to cross-border flows.

My role & contribution

  • Performed research activities as a research assistant at Politehnica University of Bucharest.
  • Participation recorded in the CV spans October 2019 to December 2021; the consortium continued until September 2023.

Project context
