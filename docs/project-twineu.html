TwinEU

Digital Twin for Europe

Project website

Project overview

TwinEU is developing a pan-European electricity-system digital twin by connecting local and regional twins through interoperable data and model exchanges. It combines physical modelling, artificial intelligence and high-performance computing to support grid operation, market coordination and renewable-energy integration. The architecture links adaptive digital twins, a trusted data-sharing layer and a service workbench for reusable applications and computing resources.

My role & contribution

  • Contributed to the project’s research activities as a senior researcher.

Project context

  • Coordinator: Fraunhofer-Gesellschaft, Germany.
  • Eight demonstrators across eleven countries address grid resilience, monitoring and operation, forecasting and market decisions, and coordinated network planning.
  • The wider collaboration spans fifteen European countries and includes POLITEHNICA Bucharest.
  • Interoperability is supported by standard interfaces, open APIs and connections to energy data spaces.
  • Funding topic: HORIZON-CL5-2023-D3-01-10.
