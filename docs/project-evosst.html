EVOSST

Evaluation and Valorization of Social Services for a Sustainable Transition

Project overview

EVOSST develops life-course assessment models for healthcare, childcare, long-term care, education and social inclusion. The consortium combines well-being measures and digital technologies to support more effective, evidence-informed social policies.

My role & contribution

  • Collecting and processing datasets relevant to social-service impact assessment.
  • Developing predictive models for the long-term effects of services in healthcare, education and social inclusion.
  • Visualising results to support interpretation and their use in public-policy development.

Project context

  • Coordinator: University of Western Macedonia, Greece.
  • Call: HORIZON-CL2-2024-TRANSFORMATIONS-01-04.
