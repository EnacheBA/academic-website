Project overview

ELECTRON developed an integrated approach to protecting electrical power and energy systems against cyber, privacy and data attacks. Its four pillars cover risk assessment and certification, anomaly detection and prevention, failure mitigation and energy restoration, and AR/VR-based personnel training.
