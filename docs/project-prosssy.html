ProSSSy

Autonomous Polymorphic Robotic Platforms for Smart City Services

Project website

Project overview

ProSSSy investigated autonomous, adaptable robotic platforms for services in smart cities. A common mobile base could carry different application modules, allowing the same robotic architecture to support health-check kiosks, visitor guidance, advertising and perimeter monitoring. Its layered design combined navigation and movement with interchangeable service applications for use in buildings and urban spaces.

My role & contribution

  • Contributed to the project’s research activities as a senior researcher.

Project context

  • Project company: LIGHTNING NET SRL, Romania.
  • Research partner: Politehnica University of Bucharest.
  • Technical scope: autonomous or assisted movement, modular applications, electronics, mechanics, IT and remote monitoring.
  • Application areas included healthcare assistance, security, tourism and education.
  • Project implementation: 36 months.
