SMARTELTER

Green Mobile Services for Smart City Energy Systems

Project website

Project overview

SMARTELTER focused on a mobile energy-service system combining lithium-ion battery platforms, photovoltaic generation, electric-vehicle charging and hot-water production. An artificial-intelligence management platform was designed to coordinate operating parameters, locations and routes while optimising electricity generation and use. The project targeted a prototype integrating hardware, software and energy management, alongside a new production unit.

My role & contribution

  • Directed the project's research and management activities.
  • Led work within the Innovative Technological Project funding framework.

Project context

  • Four planned mobile lithium-ion battery platforms: 50, 200, 300 and 500 kWh.
  • Energy services: a 0.1 MW electric water-heating system and 22 kW and 50 kW electric-vehicle chargers.
  • Solar charging: a container with deployable photovoltaic panels rated at 40 kW.
  • Prototype work covered design, construction, testing, an operating manual and an AI module.
  • Funding: European Regional Development Fund through the Competitiveness Operational Programme 2014–2020; call POC/1033/1/3.
