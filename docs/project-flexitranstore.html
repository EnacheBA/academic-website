FLEXITRANSTORE

An Integrated Platform for Increased Flexibility in Smart Transmission Grids with Storage Entities and Large Penetration of Renewable Energy Sources

Project overview

FLEXITRANSTORE developed a flexible energy-grid platform combining storage, demand response, control improvements and electricity-market mechanisms. Eight demonstrations across six countries addressed renewable integration, cross-border electricity exchange and more effective use of existing grid infrastructure.
